## Your components

<ProjectNote>A custom component is registered once and used here without an import.</ProjectNote>

Add an import and an entry to the registry. `useMDXComponents(overrides)` merges per-render overrides after the defaults. Unknown or incorrectly cased component names fail during MDX compilation when `remarkDocsComponents` is configured.
